# Inspiration and upstream projects

The editor browsing/import workflow was inspired by **Poly Haven Import** by
**Bytezz / Bytez**, https://github.com/Bytezz/godot-polyhaven-import (GPL-3.0).
We inspected that project while designing this addon. This is an independent
implementation against the official 3DTexel REST API: no Poly Haven Import source
files, artwork, icons or other assets are included or adapted here. This is not
a fork, and the two projects are not affiliated.

Godot's built-in editor theme supplies the material icon at runtime.
3DTexel is a third-party service; this community addon is not an official
3DTexel product. Service names belong to their respective owners.

Downloaded textures and assets retain their own licenses, separate from this addon's MIT
license. Each import writes its returned license and source URL in provenance.json.
Screenshots may depict CC0 materials from 3DTexel or OpenGameArt; they are not bundled asset packs.
