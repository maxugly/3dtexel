# Yale Center for British Art Attribution & Terms of Use

The Yale Center for British Art holds the largest collection of British art outside the United Kingdom and provides access to public domain artworks under an open-access policy.

- **Institution**: Yale Center for British Art (YCBA)
- **Portal**: https://collections.britishart.yale.edu/
- **API**: https://collections.britishart.yale.edu/catalog.json
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0) / Public Domain
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: Images of public domain artworks in the YCBA collection are dedicated to the public domain (CC0). Citing the artist, title of work, and Yale Center for British Art is appreciated.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted without restriction for commercial and personal game development, materials, architectural decor, canvas textures, and visual effects.
