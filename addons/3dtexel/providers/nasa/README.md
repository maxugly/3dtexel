# NASA Multimedia Provider for 3DTexel

Integrates NASA's central multimedia collection (Images, Audio, and 3D Resources) into the Godot 4.4+ Asset Library.

## Features
- **NASA Images & Planetary Photography**: Mission photos, Hubble and James Webb imagery, planetary surface textures (e.g. Blue Marble, Mars, Moon).
- **Historic NASA Audio**: Mission control transmissions (Apollo, Gemini, Mercury), rocket engine launches, astronaut communications, and planetary radio emissions (`.wav`, `.mp3`).
- **NASA 3D Models & Resources**: Spacecraft (ISS, Curiosity Rover, Perseverance, Orion, James Webb) and celestial bodies in `.glb`, `.gltf`, and `.obj`.
- **Public Domain**: Official United States Government works, generally free of copyright restrictions with author/center attribution preserved.
- **Offline / Mock Testing**: Fully testable offline with synthetic JSON fixtures.
- **Rate Pacing & Cache**: 500ms pacing between requests, ETag/Last-Modified disk caching.
