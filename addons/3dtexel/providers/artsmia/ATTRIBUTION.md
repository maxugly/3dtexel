# Minneapolis Institute of Art Attribution & Terms of Use

The Minneapolis Institute of Art (Mia) provides access to public domain artworks in its permanent collection through an open-access CC0 policy.

- **Institution**: Minneapolis Institute of Art (Mia)
- **Portal**: https://collections.artsmia.org/
- **API**: https://api.artsmia.org/v1/
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0) / Public Domain
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: Public domain images from Mia's open access collection can be freely downloaded and modified. Attribution citing the artist, artwork title, and Minneapolis Institute of Art is appreciated.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted without restriction for commercial and personal game development, materials, environmental decals, and canvas art.
