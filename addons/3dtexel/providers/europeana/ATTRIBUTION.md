# Europeana Collections Attribution

Digitized European cultural heritage items, artworks, and archival records are made available
via Europeana (https://www.europeana.eu/) under Creative Commons CC0, CC-BY, and Public Domain
provisions by participating European museums, libraries, and galleries.

The Europeana provider adapter is an independent implementation under the MIT license
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Europeana Foundation.
