# Los Angeles County Museum of Art Provider for 3DTexel

Connects 3DTexel to LACMA's open collections, featuring over 140,000 artworks spanning Asian art, Latin American art, European masterworks, American decorative art, and photography.

## Features
- **Global Artistic Heritage**: Japanese woodblocks, European master paintings, Mesoamerican ceramics, modern drawings, and historical textiles.
- **Multiple Resolutions**: High-res original scans, 2048px, 1024px, and thumbnail previews.
- **In-Game Art & Material Decals**: Perfect for museum interiors, historical architectural decorations, tapestry materials, and level design.
- **Offline Tests**: 100% offline contract and regression tests with mock client responses.
