# Wikimedia Commons Attribution

Public domain artworks, photographic media, and digitized cultural heritage assets are made
available via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/) under Public Domain Mark 1.0,
PD-Art (Public Domain - Art), and Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal provisions.

The Wikimedia Commons provider adapter is an independent implementation under the MIT license
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
