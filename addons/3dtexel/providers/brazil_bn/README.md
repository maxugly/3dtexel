# Biblioteca Nacional do Brasil Digital Provider Adapter

Integrates the Biblioteca Nacional Digital do Brasil (BNDigital) collection (rare historical maps of the Americas, colonial manuscripts, botanical engravings, and iconography) into the 3dtexel asset library.

## Capabilities
- Search historical cartography and manuscripts from South America
- Multi-tier downloads with IIIF image support (`orig`, `2048`, `1024`)
- Public Domain license compliance and Brazilian national archive provenance
- 100% offline testing with synthetic fixtures
