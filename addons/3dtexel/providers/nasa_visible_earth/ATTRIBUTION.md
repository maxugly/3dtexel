# NASA Visible Earth Attribution & Terms of Use

Visible Earth is a catalog of NASA images and animations of our home planet, produced by NASA's Earth Observatory at Goddard Space Flight Center.

- **Institution**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory
- **Portal**: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/
- **License**: Public Domain (United States Government Work, NASA Still and Motion Media Guidelines)
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: NASA imagery and earth data are in the public domain and generally not protected by copyright. Attribution to "NASA Earth Observatory / Visible Earth" and referencing specific missions (MODIS, VIIRS, Landsat) is requested.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted without restriction for games, 3D globes, planet shaders, and space rendering.
