# Te Papa Tongarewa Provider Adapter

Integrates the Museum of New Zealand Te Papa Tongarewa collections (Taonga Māori, Pacific cultures, natural history, and art) into the 3dtexel asset library.

## Capabilities
- Search cultural heritage objects and art
- High-resolution image downloads (`orig`, `2048`, `1024`)
- Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0 and Public Domain license compliance
- 100% offline testing with synthetic fixtures
