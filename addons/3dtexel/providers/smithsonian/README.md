# Smithsonian Open Access & 3D Provider for 3DTexel

Integrates the Smithsonian Institution's Open Access collections and Smithsonian 3D digitizations into the Godot 4.4+ Asset Library.

## Features
- **3D Scans**: Digitized 3D surface scans of world-renowned museum artifacts, Apollo 11 spacecraft, dinosaur and woolly mammoth fossils, historical statues, and specimens in GLB, OBJ, and STL formats.
- **2D Collections & Photography**: High-resolution CC0 artwork, paintings, and historical photography from 21 Smithsonian museums, research centers, and the National Zoo.
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) dedication.
- **Offline / Mock Testing**: Fully testable offline with synthetic JSON fixtures.
- **Rate Pacing & Cache**: 500ms pacing and response disk caching.
