# The Metropolitan Museum of Art Provider Adapter

Integrates The Met's Open Access collection (over 490,000 public domain artworks spanning 5,000 years of world culture) into the 3dtexel asset library.

## Capabilities
- Search artworks across European paintings, Asian art, arms & armor, and decorative arts
- Download original and high-resolution primary images
- CC0 1.0 Universal license compliance with complete artist provenance
- 100% offline testing with synthetic fixtures
