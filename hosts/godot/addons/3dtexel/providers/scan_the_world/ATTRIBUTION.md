# Scan The World Attribution & Terms of Use

Scan The World is an ambitious initiative creating a free, open-source archive of 3D printable and gameready sculptures, statues, and historical cultural artifacts from global museums.

- **Institution**: Scan The World & Global Partner Museums (Musée du Louvre, British Museum, Vatican Museums, The Met)
- **Portal**: https://www.myminifactory.com/scantheworld/
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) / Public Domain
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: CC0 requires no legal attribution, but crediting "Scan The World" and the originating museum is strongly encouraged to preserve provenance.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted without restriction for commercial and non-commercial games, virtual museums, architectural visualization, and 3D scenes.
