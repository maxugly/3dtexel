# USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection Attribution & Terms of Use

The USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection at the National Agricultural Library comprises 7,497 botanical watercolor illustrations created between 1886 and 1942 by USDA staff artists documenting fruit and nut specimens.

- **Institution**: United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) National Agricultural Library
- **Portal**: https://usdawatercolors.nal.usda.gov/
- **License**: Public Domain (United States Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105)
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: As a work of the United States Government, these illustrations are in the public domain and free of copyright restrictions. Attribution to the artist and the USDA National Agricultural Library is recommended.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted without restriction for games, materials, food icons, UI, and commercial projects.
