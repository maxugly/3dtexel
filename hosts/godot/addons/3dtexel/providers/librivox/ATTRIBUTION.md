# LibriVox Audiobooks Attribution & Terms of Use

LibriVox provides free, public domain audio recordings of classic literature, poetry, plays, speeches, and historical texts read by volunteers across the world.

- **Portal**: https://librivox.org/
- **API**: https://librivox.org/api/feed/audiobooks/

- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: All LibriVox recordings are in the public domain. Citing the author, reader (narrator), and LibriVox is polite and appreciated, but not legally required.
- **Commercial Use**: 100% permitted without restriction in commercial games, voiceovers, background NPC dialogue, radio broadcasts, and sound design.
