# Biblioteca Nacional do Brasil Digital Attribution

Digitized historical cartography, manuscripts, iconography, and rare documents are made available
by the Fundação Biblioteca Nacional do Brasil via https://bndigital.bn.gov.br/ under Public Domain
provisions (Domínio Público).

The Biblioteca Nacional do Brasil provider adapter is an independent implementation under the MIT license
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Fundação Biblioteca Nacional do Brasil.
