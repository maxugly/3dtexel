# Freesound Attribution & Terms of Use

Freesound is a collaborative database of Creative Commons licensed sounds, audio samples, Foley recordings, field recordings, and musical elements maintained by the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona.

- **Institution / Community**: Freesound (Universitat Pompeu Fabra)
- **Portal**: https://freesound.org/
- **API Documentation**: https://freesound.org/docs/api/
- **Licenses**: Creative Commons 0 (CC0), Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 3.0/4.0), and Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial (CC-BY-NC).
- **License Policy**:
  - Sounds under **Creative Commons 0 (CC0)** require no attribution and are permissive for all commercial/open-source game projects.
  - Sounds under **CC-BY** require crediting the sound creator and sound ID; 3DTexel automatically records this metadata in provenance and sidecar files.
  - Non-commercial sounds (CC-BY-NC) are marked as blocked for commercial distribution.
- **Commercial Use**: Commercial game audio use is permitted for CC0 and CC-BY audio assets.
