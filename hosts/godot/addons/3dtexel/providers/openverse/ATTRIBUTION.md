# Openverse Attribution & Terms of Use

Openverse is a search engine for open-access and openly-licensed media operated by the WordPress Foundation, indexing over 700 million Creative Commons licensed and public domain images and audio recordings from world museums, libraries, archives, and online communities.

- **Institution / Project**: Openverse (WordPress Foundation)
- **Portal**: https://openverse.org/
- **API Documentation**: https://api.openverse.org/v1/
- **Licenses**: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0), Public Domain Mark (PDM), Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 1.0 - 4.0), and other CC variations.
- **Attribution Policy**: Openverse returns machine-readable license and creator attribution metadata for every indexed record. 3DTexel preserves this provenance in `.credits.json` and sidecar files.
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted for CC0, Public Domain, and CC-BY indexed works without restriction.
