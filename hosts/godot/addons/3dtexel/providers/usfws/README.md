# US Fish & Wildlife Service Provider for 3DTexel

Integrates the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service (USFWS) National Digital Library into the Godot 4.4+ Asset Library.

## Features
- **Wildlife & Nature Photography**: Wild birds, raptors, mammals, aquatic life, forests, national wildlife refuges.
- **License**: Public Domain (United States Government work).
- **Offline / Mock Testing**: Fully testable offline with synthetic JSON fixtures.
- **Pacing & Cache**: 500ms pacing and response disk caching.
