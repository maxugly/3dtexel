# NOAA Coastal & Marine Provider for 3DTexel

Integrates the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Digital Coast and Ocean Service collections into the Godot 4.4+ Asset Library.

## Features
- **Coastal Elevation & Lidar**: Digital Coast elevation meshes (GLB) and bathymetric data.
- **Oceanic & Marine Photography**: Coral reefs, deep-sea expeditions, marine wildlife, satellite weather and oceanography.
- **License**: Public Domain (United States Government work).
- **Offline / Mock Testing**: Fully testable offline with synthetic JSON fixtures.
- **Pacing & Cache**: 500ms pacing and response disk caching.
