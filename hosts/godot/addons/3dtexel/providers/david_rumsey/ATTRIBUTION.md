# David Rumsey Historical Map Collection Attribution & Terms of Use

The David Rumsey Map Collection contains more than 150,000 digitized historical maps and images spanning five centuries (16th through 21st century), hosted in collaboration with Stanford University Libraries.

- **Institution**: David Rumsey Map Collection / Stanford University Libraries
- **Portal**: https://www.davidrumsey.com/
- **Catalog API**: https://www.davidrumsey.com/luna/servlet/api/

- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/

- **Commercial Use**: Public domain maps and CC0/CC-BY assets are licensed for commercial and non-commercial game development, textures, and terrain mapping.
