# MorphoSource Attribution & Terms of Use

MorphoSource is an open-access repository for 3D data representing biological specimens and cultural heritage objects, operated by Duke University in collaboration with museums and research institutions worldwide.

- **Institution**: Duke University & Global Museum Partners
- **Portal**: https://www.morphosource.org/
- **API**: https://www.morphosource.org/api/v1/find
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal / Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 4.0)
- **License URI**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Attribution Policy**: MorphoSource promotes open science. CC0 items require no attribution; CC-BY items require crediting the scanning institution (e.g. AMNH, Smithsonian, Harvard MCZ) and specimen catalog number.
- **Commercial Use**: Open CC0 and CC-BY 3D models and scans are permitted for games, anatomy studies, paleontology models, and 3D visual assets.
