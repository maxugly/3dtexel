# Museum für Kunst und Gewerbe Hamburg Provider for 3DTexel

Connects 3DTexel to MK&G Hamburg's open collection of fine art, applied arts, graphic design, photography, Islamic art, and Art Nouveau posters.

## Features
- **Graphic Design & Applied Arts**: Renowned for Jugendstil / Art Nouveau posters, historical musical instruments, pottery, and Japanese woodcuts.
- **Multiple Resolutions**: High-res original master scans, 2048px, 1024px, and thumbnail previews via IIIF.
- **In-Game Art & Material Decals**: Perfect for vintage poster designs, ornamental ceramics, period architectural ornaments, and level set dressing.
- **Offline Tests**: 100% offline contract and regression tests with mock client responses.
